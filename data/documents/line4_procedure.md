# Line 4 Operating Procedure

**Document type:** Line Operating Procedure
**Applies to:** Line 4 - Final Assembly
**Owner:** Line 4 Production Supervisor

## Overview

Line 4 (Final Assembly department) performs final component assembly. The
line runs three shifts per day (Day, Evening, Night) across four conveyor
assembler stations working in parallel, each contributing to the line's
total daily output. Planned output is allocated evenly across the four
stations per shift; the line's daily planned total is the sum of all four
stations across all three shifts.

## Shift Handover

At the start of each shift, the incoming operator must:

- Review the outgoing shift's handover notes for any flagged equipment
  issues, near-misses, or open maintenance items on any of the four
  stations.
- Confirm each station is running within its normal temperature and speed
  range before ramping to full output.
- Check the previous shift's rejection rate and dominant defect type, if
  logged, before starting the new shift's run.

## Standard Operating Parameters

- Each station targets its assigned planned quantity per shift; actual
  output below roughly 90% of planned for a station should be logged as a
  downtime or performance note even if the station never fully stopped.
- Normal per-station downtime is a small number of minutes per shift
  (routine micro-stops, changeovers). Downtime materially above that
  baseline on any single station should be investigated using the
  Downtime Investigation Checklist in the Machine Maintenance Manual,
  focusing on that specific station rather than assuming a line-wide
  cause.
- Normal per-station rejection rate is low single digits. A station
  running well above that rate, especially when the other three stations
  on the same shift are normal, points to a station-specific issue rather
  than a line-wide process issue - see the Quality Inspection Procedure
  for how to correlate a defect spike with equipment condition.

## Changeover Procedure

- Changeovers must be logged with start/end time; changeover time counts
  toward planned downtime, not unplanned downtime.
- After a changeover, run a short verification batch and check dimensional
  and assembly quality before resuming full-rate production.

## When a Single Station Underperforms

If one of Line 4's four stations shows a clear drop in actual output,
elevated downtime, or an elevated rejection rate while the other three
stations remain normal for the same shift and date, treat it as a
station-specific issue:

1. Check that station's maintenance history (Machine Maintenance Manual /
   Motor Failure SOP if a motor-driven fault is suspected).
2. Check that station's quality data for the same period (Quality
   Inspection Procedure).
3. Do not average the affected station's numbers into a line-wide
   conclusion without also reporting the station-level detail - a
   line-wide average can understate how severe a single-station issue is,
   since it is diluted by the other three normally-performing stations.

## Escalation Contacts

- Equipment or downtime issues: Maintenance shift lead
- Quality/rejection rate issues: Quality Assurance shift lead
- Output shortfalls affecting the daily production plan: Line 4 Production
  Supervisor, who should be notified before end of shift if the shortfall
  is likely to affect the next day's schedule
